Good to know.

Which currency do you take?

Prices are in US dollars, Bonaire's currency.

I'm on a cruise. How far are you from the pier?

Beachvibes Playa and Salty Crew Bonaire are on Kralendijk's main shopping street, a few minutes' walk from the cruise pier.

Can you put something aside for me?

Yes. Send us a WhatsApp message with what you'd like and which store you'll visit, and we'll keep it at the counter.

Do you ship online orders?

Not at the moment. Everything is sold in our three stores on Bonaire.

Is Sorobon open on Sundays?

Yes. Beachvibes Sorobon is open every day from 10:00 to 17:00. Playa and Salty Crew are closed on Sundays.

How do I order a handmade bracelet?

Design it on our jewelry page and send it via WhatsApp, or pick your colours at the counter in Beachvibes Playa.
