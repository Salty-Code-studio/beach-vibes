Who is responsible

Salty Code Studio is responsible for the data collected on this preview. Questions or requests: info@saltycodestudio.com.

What we collect, and why

We use PostHog, an analytics service with servers in the EU, to see how the preview is used: which pages are opened, for how long, the approximate country and city, the type of device and browser, and the page you came from. We use this to follow up on the design and improve it. We do not store your IP address.

If you opened a personal link (one ending in ?for= and a name), visits are linked to that name, so we know the person we sent it to has seen the preview. We tell you this when the link opens.

Our legal basis is legitimate interest: presenting and improving a design proposal.

No cookies, no recordings

This preview sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device for analytics. We do not record sessions. The sign-up form is a demo and sends nothing.

How long we keep it

Visit data is kept for as long as the design project runs and deleted within 12 months.
